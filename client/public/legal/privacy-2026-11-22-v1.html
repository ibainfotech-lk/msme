Noteto — Privacy Notice

Draft version: 2026-11-22-v1 · IBA InfoTech, Jaffna, Sri Lanka

Pre-launch draft for customer testing. This notice requires a verified privacy contact, confirmed hosting and subprocessors, retention schedule and legal review, including the Sri Lankan Personal Data Protection Act as applicable from its effective dates, before public launch. English draft only; approved translations pending.

1. Who handles your information

Noteto is developed by IBA InfoTech in Jaffna, Sri Lanka. The final notice must identify the registered data controller and a working privacy contact address before production release.

2. What information is collected

When you register, we collect mobile number, OTP verification status, name, email, business name, registration type, preferred language, selected plan, and records of Terms acceptance and Privacy Notice acknowledgment. If you use the service, it also processes bookkeeping records, invoices, payments and other information you enter, plus necessary technical/security logs.

3. Why information is processed

We use this information to authenticate users, set up and manage accounts, provide bookkeeping functions, protect against abuse, manage permitted subscriptions and payments, answer support requests and satisfy applicable legal obligations. We do not treat Privacy Notice acknowledgment as permission to send optional marketing.

4. Legal basis and choices

The applicable basis may include providing the service, legal obligations, legitimate interests where permitted, and separate consent where required. Optional marketing requires a distinct choice and should offer an easy opt-out. Declining optional marketing does not prevent registration.

5. Sharing and service providers

Necessary service providers may include hosting, SMS/OTP delivery, payment services, email communications and secure storage. We do not authorize third-party advertising merely by accepting this notice. The final notice must list actual processors, data locations and safeguards, including any overseas transfers.

6. Retention and security

We use appropriate technical and organizational protections, including business-level access controls. We retain information as necessary for service provision, record-keeping, dispute handling and applicable laws. Specific retention periods and deletion schedules must be finalized and published before public launch.

7. Your requests

You may ask how we use your information and request appropriate corrections or other actions available under applicable law. The final privacy contact and instructions for submitting requests must be published before launch. Rights and remedies apply when the relevant law provisions are in force.

8. Updates

When the Notice materially changes, a new dated version will be published. Where a fresh acknowledgment or consent is required, it will be requested separately.
