## Why this is review-only

The Sri Lankan Inland Revenue Department (IRD) states that income-tax years of assessment begin on **1 April** and income may come from business, employment, investment and other sources. A business cashbook does not hold all of those types, personal reliefs, capital adjustments or allowable tax deductions. A sole proprietor is not automatically eligible for a business tax exemption merely because an individual's personal relief applies in certain circumstances.

Official references checked 24 November 2026:

- IRD income-tax overview and Inland Revenue (Amendment) Act 11 of 2026: https://www.ird.gov.lk/en/Type%20of%20Taxes/SitePages/Income%20Tax.aspx?menuid=1201
- IRD 2025/2026 tax chart (distinguishes individuals, ordinary companies, partnerships, NGOs, charities and special incomes): https://www.ird.gov.lk/en/publications/SitePages/Tax_Chart_2526.aspx?menuid=140407
- IRD August 2026 re-revised circular concerning 2026/2027 quarterly tax installments, SEC/2026/E/06: https://www.ird.gov.lk/en/publications/sitepages/circulars.aspx?menuid=1506

The IRD tax chart refers to eligible individuals' LKR 1,800,000 personal relief for 2025/2026, **not a universal zero-tax rule** for businesses or for cash profits below that amount. The chart lists special rates and exceptions. No statutory rate or relief is silently applied to company, sole-proprietor or community-organization users. The IRD page also lists amendments enacted June 2026, so no 2025/2026 rate is assumed to govern all of 2026/2027 without legal review.

## What F.17.4a actually provides

- An on-demand, read-only **tax-preparation cashbook summary**, not a filed or final tax assessment.
- Year of assessment `YYYY/YYYY+1`, from **April 1** through **March 31** (e.g. 2026/2027 is 2026-04-01 to 2027-03-31).
- Shows cash already received, cash already paid, net cash movements and counts **as recorded**, including negative surplus; the report covers only entries through the latest Colombo calendar date.
- Uses MySQL `DECIMAL` aggregation converted into `BigInt` cents to avoid floating-point precision errors.
- Excludes unpaid invoices because they are not recorded cash receipts.
- Discloses the business's stored registration type: `SOLE_PROPRIETOR`, `PRIVATE_LIMITED`, or `COMMUNITY_ORGANIZATION`.
- Returns **null**, never 0, for `taxableIncomeLkr`, `taxPayableLkr`, `paymentDueLkr`, `personalReliefAppliedLkr` and `taxRatePercent`. No source is misrepresented as tax-ready when only cash entries exist.
- Requires qualified Sri Lankan tax review and confirmation of taxpayer identity, other personal/business income, exempt receipts, deductible expenses, capital allowances, withheld credits, business registration and applicable effective-dated legislation.
- No background automatic tax computation, invoice or subscription-payment debit.

## Security and API

Read-only route:

`GET /api/taxes/businesses/:businessId/readiness?assessmentYearStart=2026`

Validation and access:
- API/service require `NODE_ENV=development` **and** `NOTETO_F17_TAX_READINESS_ENABLED=true`.
- Authenticated server-side user session, active membership, exact business-scoped role and `taxes.read` permission.
- Business active, current ACTIVE/TRIALING subscription with effective dates and no held timestamp; existing `GENERATE_REPORT` access policy is checked.
- Invalid or future assessment years rejected before DB access; SQL always filters `business_id=?` and year range.
- No mutations permitted (other methods return HTTP 405 under private development guard). Never creates/edits tax rules, grants, charges, tax statements, cash transactions or saved reports.

React UI requires Vite development mode plus `VITE_NOTETO_F17_BOOKKEEPING_UI_ENABLED=true` and `VITE_NOTETO_F17_TAX_READINESS_UI_ENABLED=true`, with English/Tamil/Sinhala explanations. A frontend flag cannot grant server authorization.

## CI and safeguards

The `test:mysql-tax-readiness` suite executes against an explicitly guarded disposable MySQL 8.4 service, after migrations and existing F17 tests; migration count remains **21**.

Tests verify:
- April–March year boundaries (2025/26 and 2026/27).
- Exact LKR received/paid/surplus, negative values and entry counts.
- All three registration types always require review and return null tax payable.
- Unpaid invoices never count as cash income.
- Tenant isolation, `taxes.read` RBAC, current trial, held/expired/suspended account denial.
- Invalid year and future selection, strict HTTP query, no write routes, and production gate rejecting before any DB connection.

## Required F.17.4b before showing an estimated tax figure

1. Tax professional confirms entity classification, who is taxable (owner/person versus company or community entity), period and residency.
2. Source-supported, **effective-dated, reviewer-approved** tax rule sets with immutable version, source URLs, valid-from/to, brackets/special income categories, rounding and approval evidence.
3. Explicit user-provided verified personal or entity-wide income, reliefs, exempt receipts, capital allowances, deductions and tax credits.
4. Taxable profit reconciliation from book surplus, distinguishing capital assets and nondeductible expenses from ordinary cash expenses.
5. Deterministic reviewed estimates marked as estimates, with case-specific statutory overrides, amendments and limitations, never automatic government tax filing/payment.
6. Legal review, load/security test, customer pilot and production configuration approval.

**Green CI means development-level behavior passed; it does not validate legal or tax accuracy and does not authorize public release.**
