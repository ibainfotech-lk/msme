## Proposed controlled work order

1. **F.17.5b — Production service boundary design (no deploy):** Separate production vs disposable development settings; map DB/migration, session, proxy, secrets, telemetry, CSRF/abuse, OTP/provider interfaces. Author architecture/security tests without weakening fail-closed environments.
2. **F.17.5c — OTP provider contract & test harness:** Choose approved provider and privacy terms; build adapter interface with signed/verified delivery responses, abuse/expiry controls and synthetic tests. Never send real messages in CI.
3. **F.17.5d — Commercial Bank payment integration design:** Obtain official merchant onboarding and current technical documentation; map gateway initiation/signatures, customer redirect, webhook authenticity, server verification and reconciliation. No customer charge until independently approved. Avoid guessing bank endpoint formats.
4. **F.17.5e — Trial/promo activation design:** Reserve → verify eligibility/identity → atomically activate only authorized zero-price grant; reconcile pending paid attempts; ensure unique entitlements and idempotency.
5. **F.17.5f — Controlled production entitlement + bookkeeping/invoicing/reporting gates:** Independent financial/accounting and security reviews, privilege controls, migration rollout and safe pilot.
6. **F.17.6 — Pilot acceptance & release engineering:** UAT in real devices/languages, negative flows, monitoring, incident response, backups/restore, rollback, release checklist and human go/no-go.

The names above are proposed planning sub-milestones, not approved or implemented work.

## Minimum production acceptance matrix

- **Onboarding:** phone OTP arrives and verifies under abuse controls; same phone/idempotency cannot create duplicate activations; required email and localized consent; pending order resumes safely.
- **Billing:** signed Commercial Bank initiation and callback/webhook verification; duplicate/reordered/missing callback reconciliation; no double charge, early activation or false success; refunds and customer support documented.
- **Free offer:** one eligible 3-month trial per verified identity; limited unique 1/3/6-month promotion codes; payment and trial cannot both grant duplicate benefits; post-trial billing needs transparent opt-in/terms.
- **Core cashbook:** income/expense creation, historical audited edits, business-scoped RBAC, HOLD restrictions and backups; no phantom cash from unpaid invoices.
- **Invoice/report:** issue/number/limit at 30/month as approved, monthly warnings at 28, authorized reporting/export and disclaimers; verify invoice legal requirements with qualified accountant.
- **Operations:** production DB isolation, least privilege, monitoring, tested backups/restore, vulnerability review, key rotation, data retention/privacy policies, admin incident controls, deployment rollback.
- **Acceptance:** synthetic automated CI; sandbox gateway tests; reviewed disposable-db integration; manual end-to-end pilot with written sign-off before deployment.

## Hard stops

- Do **not** switch `NODE_ENV` or remove development guards to force features into production.
- Do **not** wire a mock payment gateway or fake callback to a production success/activation path.
- Do **not** create paid entitlement, free-trial subscription or monthly invoice entitlement from a pending/unverified attempt.
- Do **not** migrate or mutate local/production customer databases as part of this planning milestone.
- Do **not** represent tax payable or professional tax approval as available at launch.
- Do **not** merge `main` or deploy because a documentation branch's CI is green.
