## Why this milestone has no tax rates

The **Inland Revenue (Amendment) Act No. 11 of 2026** introduced changes with varying commencement dates. IRD also published a **re-revised circular SEC/2026/E/06 (12 August 2026)** for quarterly instalment calculations from Y/A 2026/27. Therefore, effective dates, taxpayer category, legal source, amendments, and qualified review all matter. A business's LKR cashbook net movement does not establish total assessable/taxable income. Personal tax relief is **not automatically a business-wide allowance**.

Official sources (verified 24 November 2026):
- [IRD Income Tax](https://www.ird.gov.lk/en/Type%20of%20Taxes/SitePages/Income%20Tax.aspx?menuid=1201)
- [IRD Circulars](https://www.ird.gov.lk/en/publications/sitepages/circulars.aspx?menuid=1506), including SEC/2026/E/06 (Re-Revised)
- [IRD 2025/26 tax chart](https://www.ird.gov.lk/en/publications/SitePages/Tax_Chart_2526.aspx?menuid=140407)
- [Inland Revenue (Amendment) Act No. 11 of 2026](https://www.ird.gov.lk/en/publications/Acts_Income%20Tax_2017/IR_Act_No_11-2026_E.pdf)

These references document the legal review process; they are **not a blanket rule set approved for every customer**. No live rate, threshold, tax due or relief is seeded or calculated in this milestone.

## Data model and migration

New additive migration: `20261124_032_tax_rule_source_reviews`; total migrations become **22**.

Table `tax_rule_source_reviews` stores:
- Unique source code + revision, taxpayer scope (individual/company/other entity).
- Assessment year start and effective-from/to dates.
- Source title, official HTTPS URL and SHA-256 of the independently reviewed source document.
- `DRAFT` (default), `REVIEWED` or `REVOKED`.
- Reviewer user ID, review date, note and creation date.

MySQL enforces positive versions, year range, effective-date ordering, and a reviewer/date requirement for reviewed or revoked records. The table is **not a taxpayer rule calculation schema**: no rates, brackets, relief entitlements or computation instructions are stored. There is **no user/admin HTTP write endpoint**, no automatic legal-source ingestion and no implicit approval. A later separately reviewed, access-controlled administrative procedure must own source verification, approval, revocation, audit logging, and any further tax-rule schema.

A row marked REVIEWED is a **source document status only**. It does **not** mean a taxpayer's eligibility or income adjustments have been verified.

## Read-only API and review controls

`GET /api/taxes/businesses/:businessId/sources?assessmentYearStart=2026`

Uses the existing F.17.4a `viewTaxReadiness` business/subscription lock, active membership, `taxes.read` permission and `GENERATE_REPORT` hold guard, with the source lookup performed in the same transaction.

- Requires `NODE_ENV=development` and `NOTETO_F17_TAX_READINESS_ENABLED=true`; production returns 503.
- Only `REVIEWED` records for the chosen year that overlap the April–March assessment dates can appear; drafts, revoked and other-year dates are excluded.
- Source links must be HTTPS from `ird.gov.lk` or `parliament.lk` and subdomains; malformed or nonofficial sources fail closed.
- Returns a maximum of 50 official references; any excess fails closed, rather than providing misleading partial coverage.
- Response contains reviewed legal references only and explicitly sets `taxableIncomeLkr:null`, `taxPayableLkr:null`, `taxCalculationEnabled:false`, `taxpayerEligibilityConfirmed:false` and `reviewRequired:true`.
- All mutating HTTP methods remain denied.

The private multilingual React tax-readiness screen adds a legal-source section after the cash-movement summary, without suggesting that a viewed official reference validates the customer's tax position.
