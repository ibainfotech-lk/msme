## 3. Vendor information request / decision worksheet

For EACH candidate, obtain written evidence for:

1. **Commercial eligibility:** legal company/merchant registration requirements, named account manager, transactional OTP contract and billing; current quoted currency, price per delivered/accepted SMS or segment, setup/minimum/monthly fees, tax, sender-ID charges and refund rules.
2. **Sender ID:** `NOTETO` branding eligibility and registration evidence, expected operator-by-operator approval, any brand mention requirements and testing rights. **No demo sender for OTP**.
3. **Coverage:** independent test lines across Dialog, SLT-Mobitel, Hutch and Airtel, real Unicode delivery in Tamil/Sinhala, time-to-deliver percentiles and delays/failure handling.
4. **API security:** official current send/status/error schema, TLS policy, header authentication versus body credentials, access control/IP allowlist, scopes, key rotation, throttling, request idempotency, webhook/status authenticity and payload retention.
5. **Sandbox:** dedicated restricted credentials, synthetic or expressly authorized test numbers, safe quotas, explicit charges, outbound allowlist, delivery simulation, stable response examples and non-production sender.
6. **Operational controls:** provider uptime/service level and maintenance notices, abuse/rate limits, available support SLAs, provider failure/retry semantics, message-id reconciliation and manual incident escalation.
7. **Legal/privacy:** provider/operator/subprocessor terms, phone-number and message-content handling, data residency, retention/deletion, access rights, lawful basis and breach notice. Independent legal review is required.
8. **Security sign-off:** approved credential handling in a secret manager, no secrets in frontend, CI, GitHub, docs, tickets, logs or this document; redacted delivery metrics, idempotency and controlled retries.

Provide **quotation date**, document/version, provider contact, assessor, decision, unresolved risk and sign-off. Do not invent approvals or dates.

## 4. Explicit technical decisions needed for Noteto

- Normalize Sri Lankan mobile numbers in one canonical server-side format. Keep `+94...` in the current user database; adapt only at provider boundary when necessary.
- Keep **Noteto-generated one-time OTP** for the first live adapter unless separately redesigning the authentication model. The SMS provider only transports the message; a provider delivery receipt or callback does not prove OTP possession.
- Preserve existing five-minute one-time challenge expiry and five-attempt verification cap until approved change. F.17.5d proposed 30-second cooldown and per-source quotas were synthetic policy assumptions, not deployed distributed limits.
- Do not create a challenge and claim delivery succeeded on a provider timeout. Define transactional outbox and durable retry/reconciliation policy, especially when the provider accepted a message but the response was lost.
- Never reuse an old OTP after a resend without a clearly reviewed rotation/version model. Never log OTP plaintext or phone numbers to shared telemetry, even on exceptions.
- Prevent anonymous OTP abuse: distributed atomic counters with window lengths, per-phone/IP/device risk, acceptable recovery path, suppression handling and uniform public responses to avoid account enumeration.
- Keep both production `/request-otp` and `/verify-otp` blocked until signed-off delivery, secure receipt handling, session and origin/CSRF checks and test evidence exist.
- Trial/promo and payment-first registration remain separate; successful OTP does **not** mean payment verified, subscription active or business eligible.

## 5. Synthetic and sandbox acceptance gates

**Synthetic CI (no SMS/network):** missing/invalid provider config; phone normalization; wrong language/Unicode billing; sender-id mismatch; unauthorized modes; malformed/forged/duplicate responses; provider timeout; API 401/403/429/5xx; exact retries/idempotency; no account enumeration; redact all secrets, phone numbers and OTPs; verify production routes remain disabled.

**Provider sandbox (only after approval):** use sanctioned test recipients and secret-managed sandbox credentials; verify acknowledgment versus delivery and OTP challenge completion are distinct; delayed, duplicate and out-of-order messages; no duplicate costs or phantom sessions after retries; source-specific circuit breakers, provider outage fallback to no login, and audit logs with no sensitive payloads.

**Restricted pilot (later separate approval):** written consent and privacy clearance, approved `NOTETO` Sender ID, cross-operator SMS tests, three-language message review, measured OTP success/latency and spend caps, CSRF/rate-limiting penetration tests, support runbook, rollback strategy, monitoring and executive go/no-go.

The approved provider's current API documentation must be checked before committing a real provider adapter. Do not implement a guessed endpoint.
