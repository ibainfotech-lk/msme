# Noteto F.17.4e — Professional Tax Verification: Design & Threat Model (Private Development)

**Base checkpoint:** F.17.4d, commit `c14ea1a3f5508e67f8cbad5a72c42d6a1568fd98`, GitHub Actions workflow #48 (three jobs green on 2026-11-24, per operator evidence).  
**Branch:** `feature/f17-4e-professional-verification-design`.  
**Milestone scope:** Design and review criteria **only**. This commit adds **no runnable tax-approval code, no migration, no API route, no permission grant, no tax rule, and no tax amount**. The development-only F17.4 tax gates remain in force. There are still 23 existing migrations.

## 1. Non-negotiable constraints

- F.17.4c intake answers and F.17.4d checklist items are **SELF_DECLARED_UNVERIFIED**, including when 7/7 are answered. Treat `UNKNOWN` and `NOT_SURE` as unanswered.
- `taxableIncomeLkr`, `taxPayableLkr`, and `paymentDueLkr` remain `null`, never a manufactured zero or a computation. Nothing in this design authorizes filings, payments, tax-invoice issuance, adjustments, or reliefs.
- Existing `taxes.read` and `taxes.manage` permissions do not imply professional-review permission. Do not alter the approved 12-permission registration-owner baseline; do not give reviewer permissions to all owners, admins, or system roles.
- Reuse server-checked business membership, tenant ID, active/trial subscription, HOLD and suspended/expired restrictions. All future gates must be enforced on the server inside the decision transaction, not inferred from UI controls, role names, request claims or an unverified email.
- Never let source document review (F17.4b) be interpreted as customer-specific verification or as an approved rate schedule.
- Keep this milestone development-only. No data access or feature flag is to be enabled in production.

## 2. Distinct actors and separation of duties (proposal, not live RBAC)

| Actor | Future bounded capability | Must not be able to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Business declarant | Update the tenant's unverified F17.4c questionnaire under existing `taxes.manage` and see their F17.4d checklist under `taxes.read` | Approve or verify their own claims |
| Authorized reviewer | After explicit, time-bound delegation and credential validation, inspect minimal scoped evidence and record an independently sourced **review recommendation** | Approve their own recommendation; change cashbook data, subscriptions or payments |
| Independent approver | After separate authorization, review a particular recommendation plus bound evidence and accept/reject it | Approve when also the declarant, submitting reviewer, direct beneficiary, or conflicted party |
| Security/data administrator | Provision and revoke narrowly scoped authorizations through separately audited operations | Substitute for a tax reviewer, override tax conclusions or bypass segregation-of-duties checks |

Eligibility for any professional review role, its authorized practice scope in Sri Lanka and contractual responsibilities must be approved by qualified legal/tax advisers before implementation. No present-day `OWNER` role should inherit a reviewer capability.

## 3. Proposed future workflow (not implemented)

1. **Consent & access request:** Show purpose, exact assessment year, requested evidence classes, access scope, retention period, applicable notices and language. Create no evidence collection until privacy/legal review approves this design.
2. **Reviewer assignment:** Check verified reviewer authorization, active scoped delegation, conflict-of-interest declaration, expiry and revocation; business owner cannot self-assign privileged powers.
3. **Evidence preparation:** Keep evidence in protected storage separate from public questionnaire drafts. Store pointers and SHA-256 digests; enforce business/assessment-year scope, server-side access controls, encryption, retention limits, audit trail and malware scanning. Do not expose identity/tax numbers via logs or API responses.
4. **Independent fact checks:** Classify taxpayer legal identity and residency, owner liability, other income, assets/capital treatment, potentially nondeductible costs, and withholding credits. Reviewers record source-backed findings **per item**; unresolved/missing/conflicting evidence blocks positive verification.
5. **Recommendation:** Reviewer submits a versioned, append-only recommendation bound to the exact tenant, assessment year, intake-draft revision/digest, reviewed evidence digests and legal-source versions. Recommend `NEEDS_INFORMATION`, `RECOMMEND_VERIFIED`, or `REJECTED`, never a tax amount.
6. **Independent decision:** Separately authorized, non-conflicted approver checks all bindings, authorization and revocation **again** under transaction locks; append an immutable decision. Any missing check fails closed. Decisions are not a substitute for specific statutory-rule and tax-base review.
7. **Re-review:** New declaration revision, changed source evidence, registration/residency updates, reviewer authorization revocation, material cashbook correction, or legal-source supersession marks earlier review **STALE/RE_REVIEW_REQUIRED** by an append-only invalidation event. No old decision automatically authorizes a new draft or year.

## 4. Proposed state model (design only)

`UNSUBMITTED -> AWAITING_INFORMATION -> UNDER_REVIEW -> RECOMMENDED -> INDEPENDENTLY_REVIEWED`  
Alternative outcomes: `REJECTED`, `REVOKED`, `STALE_RE_REVIEW_REQUIRED`, `EXPIRED`.

**Important:** `INDEPENDENTLY_REVIEWED` is only an evidence/identity checkpoint, **not** `TAX_ENGINE_ENABLED`, `TAX_PAYABLE_APPROVED`, or `RETURN_AUTHORIZED`. Any positive decision is scoped to its exact draft revision, evidence set, assessment year and rule-source versions. Approvals cannot be inferred from counts, timestamps or claims. Revocation and supersession are append-only; never edit history in place.

## 5. Proposed future data contracts — NOT schema changes

- `tax_review_access_grants`: `id`, `business_id`, `reviewer_user_id`, `scope`, `assessment_year_start`, `granted_by`, `valid_from/to`, `revoked_at`; immutable grant/revoke audit events and no global or implicit privilege.
- `tax_review_evidence_refs`: `id`, `business_id`, `assessment_year_start`, `classification`, `object_key`, `sha256`, `consent_version`, `retention_policy_version`, `submitted_by`, `created_at`; never store plaintext identifiers in the current F17.4c tables.
- `tax_review_recommendation_events`: `id`, `business_id`, `assessment_year_start`, `draft_id`, `draft_revision`, `claims_sha256`, `evidence_set_sha256`, `legal_sources_sha256`, `reviewer_user_id`, `outcome`, `reason_code`, `created_at`.
- `tax_review_decision_events`: same binding fields plus `recommendation_id`, `approver_user_id`, `decision`, `conflict_check_version`, `created_at`; append-only event ID and idempotency key unique within the business/operation.
- `tax_review_invalidation_events`: `id`, `business_id`, `assessment_year_start`, `decision_id`, `cause`, `trigger_source_id`, `created_at`.
- Every future cross-record relationship must enforce tenant-scoped foreign keys or equivalent transaction-validated keys; add indexes, row-level ownership checks and concurrency controls in a separately reviewed migration. A proposed schema is **not** an authorization to create tables.
- Define explicit evidence deletion, retention and hold policies, including how audit integrity is preserved after legally required erasure. Do not select retention durations without qualified legal approval.

## 6. API boundaries for a later implementation

No new endpoint ships in F17.4e. If separately approved, use strict `z.strictObject` inputs, authenticated server session, origin/CSRF protections for mutations, explicit feature flags, short-lived scoped review grants, authorization re-checks, optimistic revisions and idempotency. Status-changing operations must lock business, authorization, draft revision and recommendation in a consistent order, atomically validate and append the decision, and reject stale/conflicting inputs. Never add a public `approve=true` query/body shortcut, client-assigned actor IDs, fallback access on missing membership, or general admin override.

Existing routes retain their boundaries:
- `GET /api/taxes/businesses/:businessId/review-checklist?assessmentYearStart=YYYY` — F17.4d read-only, unverified only.
- `GET/PUT /api/taxes/businesses/:businessId/intake` — F17.4c read and development-only unverified drafts.
- No approval endpoint, no tax calculation endpoint and no automated filing/payment operation.

## 7. Required security and acceptance tests before implementation can be approved

| Scenario | Expected future behavior |
| --- | --- |
| All seven claims supplied | Remains unverified without evidence and independent decision |
| Reviewer is the declarant, approver, or conflicted owner | Reject self-approval |
| Missing/expired/revoked grant, unverified professional credentials | Reject before evidence access or decision |
| Active reviewer attempts other business's record | Reject; no cross-tenant metadata leak |
| Business `PAST_DUE`, held, suspended, expired or production feature disabled | Reject both evidence read and decision |
| Assessment year, draft revision, evidence digest or legal-source version mismatch | Reject as stale; require new review |
| Two simultaneous approvals or replayed idempotency key | Exactly one valid decision event, no divergent outcomes |
| Evidence tampering, missing receipt/consent or unapproved retention notice | Reject |
| Cash edit or questionnaire change after recommendation | Invalidate/require re-review before any downstream use |
| Reviewer grant revoked during approval race | Serialize/re-check; revoked actor cannot approve |
| Unreviewed rates, eligibility, adjustments, personal reliefs or tax engine absent | Tax amounts remain `null`; no filing/payment |

Acceptance gate for a *future* implementation: approved privacy/evidence policy, professional-role eligibility, documented threat model, peer-reviewed migration and query plans, pure policy tests, disposable MySQL 8.4 security/integration tests, React build, manual security review and separately authorized launch decision. Production is **not** a permissible target of this design milestone.

5. Require a fresh green CI run for each increment; no merge/deployment merely because this document is committed.

**F.17.4e completion means the design is written for review, NOT that a professional verification workflow exists or any taxpayer has been verified.**
