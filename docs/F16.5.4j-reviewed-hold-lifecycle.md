# Noteto F.16.5.4j — Read-only hold and restoration review policy

**Status:** Development-only decision policy, based on the CI-validated F.16.5.4i commit `3a3c05c`. No database migrations, new routes, automatic holds, real bank payments, production rollout or activation/reinstatement are included.

## Customer outcome intended

A customer's subscription can be placed on **HOLD** only after approved terms and a documented review establish the debt and allowed grace period. While on hold, they may edit their contact profile and earlier entries, view previous reports and download preserved PDFs; they cannot add new income, expenses or invoices or generate fresh reports.

When all outstanding, billable monthly dues are settled using **verified** payment allocations and unresolved disputes are resolved, the account may be reviewed for restoring full access. Simply starting a checkout or reporting a payment is insufficient.

## Read-only decisions

The policy at `server/src/modules/subscriptions/subscription-hold-lifecycle.policy.ts` accepts **trusted server-side evidence only** and returns either a blocked code or `REVIEW_READY`. It never updates SQL and never runs a payment gateway. A future transaction service, not this policy, would need to apply reviewed status changes atomically and record them.

### Place hold (ACTIVE → PAST_DUE)

Require all of the following:
- Current ACTIVE business and ACTIVE subscription (do not hold TRIALING automatically).
- Verified monthly due history is complete through the statement date; quote is fresh, internally consistent and no future charges are inferred.
- Outstanding balance is greater than LKR 0.00.
- Verified payment reconciliation is complete; no unresolved payment attempts, disputes or chargebacks.
- No unresolved introductory trial or promotional free-month entitlement.
- Notice was delivered with a reference, and the **agreed, separately recorded** grace deadline has passed. The code does **not** invent a 3/7/14 day grace period.
- An identifiable internal reviewer approved the decision.

### Restore (PAST_DUE → ACTIVE)

Require all of the following:
- Current ACTIVE business and PAST_DUE subscription with a trustworthy hold timestamp.
- Fresh, complete and verified monthly history; total outstanding LKR 0.00.
- No unresolved payment attempts, chargebacks, disputes, or entitlement issues.
- Internal reviewer approval and identity.

A customer may still owe January and February after paying only January. With LKR 499/month, the remaining LKR 499 must be fully settled **if both months are actually due and billable** before the restoration decision is ready. Late penalties remain LKR 0.00 until a separately reviewed and customer-agreed policy exists.

## Security and delivery boundaries

**Important:** The policy expects trusted evidence; booleans such as `monthlyHistoryReviewed`, `reviewApproved` and `hasDisputesOrChargebacks` must never be accepted from browser requests, route parameters or customer-supplied JSON.

Before allowing status transitions, build and review:
1. Transactional, business-scoped evidence reader with consistent business/subscription locks, actual verified allocations and unsettled gateway attempts.
2. Evidence-backed grace/notice records and entitlement checks, including promotions, free trials and disputed invoices.
3. Audited internal approval authority, idempotent transition events and linked actor/decision evidence, with safe automatic restoration **only** after confirmed payments where appropriate.
4. Appropriate billing dates, customer notices, complaints/disputes process, security controls, observability and rollback plans.
5. CI end-to-end tests for double-submit, concurrent payment allocation, unknown/failed gateway results, past-due historical edits and state changes.

The current project also has disabled transaction-creation/report-generation APIs and development-only hold screens. CI success on this review policy alone does not mean Noteto is ready for production.
