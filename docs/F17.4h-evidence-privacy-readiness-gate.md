## 3. Minimum-evidence matrix for professional review (all categories provisional)

| F.17.4d checklist field | Potential question for reviewer | Current Noteto handling | Proposed future evidence rule |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Potential taxpayer | Who is the correct legal taxpayer? | Self-declared enum only | Professional defines minimum lawful verification; avoid full identity copies by default |
| Tax residency | What residency status applies for the relevant assessment year? | Self-declared enum only | Expert-approved source, period and decision record |
| Owner personally liable | Who carries tax responsibility? | Self-declared yes/no | Validate legal structure; avoid equating registration type with tax identity |
| Other income | Is there income outside recorded cashbook? | Self-declared yes/no | Ask for only the relevant summarized material under approved policy |
| Capital assets | Are furniture/equipment or capital allowances relevant? | Self-declared yes/no | Classify potential fixed assets separately from ordinary expenses; no assumed deduction |
| Possibly nondeductible costs | Which costs require separate treatment? | Self-declared yes/no | Document reviewer reasoning; do not auto-deduct or change cash entries |
| Withholding/credits | Are any actual withheld amounts or credits evidenced? | Self-declared yes/no | Require verifiable tax evidence before eligibility; do not imply payment or relief |

**No amounts, TINs, NIC/passport numbers, personal bank statements or original tax documents should be added to the existing F.17.4c questionnaire.** Do not put real evidence, identity numbers, credentials, secrets or customer-specific records into GitHub, CI logs, telemetry, test fixtures or screenshots.

## 4. Proposed trust boundaries (architecture only)

1. Business owner/declarant sees only the current tenant's F.17.4c unverified questionnaire and F.17.4d checklist via existing server access checks. Existing owner `taxes.manage` is **not** a professional review grant.
2. Independent reviewer and separate approver would need separately provisioned, time-limited, tenant-scoped grants, credentials checked from trusted sources, explicit consent/legal basis and audit traces. No self-provisioning, owner shortcut or global admin override.
3. Any future evidence storage is isolated from existing bookkeeping and public app assets: encrypted objects, server-managed access, short-lived retrieval, malware scanning, access logs that exclude sensitive payloads, defined key ownership and deletion handling.
4. Future recommendation, decision and invalidation history must be append-only and cryptographically bound to tenant, assessment year, draft revision, evidence set and legally reviewed source versions. An evidence SHA-256 alone does **not** prove authenticity or lawful collection.
5. A single server-side transactional decision must re-check membership, HOLD/subscription, reviewer grant/credential, conflicts, latest questionnaire version, consent/retention, evidence integrity and invalidation history. A pure-policy function does not perform those checks.
6. A future professional review still does **not** enable statutory tax computations, filings, payments or reliefs. Deterministic tax engine review and approved IRD test cases remain a separate later milestone.

## 5. Threat model and required rejection tests for later implementation

| Threat | Fail-closed behavior to prove |
| --- | --- |
| Cross-tenant evidence ID guessing | No content, metadata or permission leakage |
| Unverified, expired or revoked professional access | Reject before evidence retrieval or decision |
| Owner or reviewer attempts self-approval | Reject irrespective of frontend role claims |
| User sends a fabricated evidence hash or approval flag | Server derives trusted state; reject client-supplied authority |
| Consent withdrawn, legal basis no longer valid, or retention expiry | Restrict use, trigger policy-reviewed deletion/re-review, preserve only legally required audit evidence |
| New draft revision, cash edit or legal-source change | Mark previous decision stale; require independent re-review |
| Two concurrent approvals or replayed request | Locked transaction and idempotent append-only decision; no divergent outcomes |
| Missing evidence object, altered digest or incomplete event stream | Reject; never silently treat missing as valid |
| Held/expired subscription or suspended business | Deny tax-related review access per approved restrictions |
| Production feature disabled | Reject, not fallback to development paths |

Tests must use synthetic fixtures in a disposable environment; never use real tax/customer evidence in CI. Peer-reviewed schema changes and security testing would be separately authorized only after legal and professional readiness.

## 6. Decisions requiring explicit human sign-off

1. **Tax professional:** Who can serve as reviewer and independent approver? What minimum proof is necessary for each of the seven declarations? What circumstances mandate re-review?
2. **Legal/privacy adviser:** Which lawful basis and notices apply? What retention/deletion/access rights and cross-border rules are required? How should withdrawal and disputes work?
3. **Security reviewer:** Approved storage vendor/region, encryption/key custody, audit logging, malware scanning, backup, incident plan and attack/test checklist.
4. **Product owner:** Go/no-go for collecting evidence and integrating reviewers; approve cost, operational support and customer communications.
5. **Engineering reviewer:** Design and migration review for a separately approved implementation increment; no role grants, schema deploys or endpoints before sign-off.

Record the reviewer, decision date, approval document/reference, scope, version, unresolved objections and revocation date for each sign-off. No signature or decision is fabricated by this document.
