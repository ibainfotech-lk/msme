# Noteto F.16.5.4l — Notice and grace evidence foundation

**Development-only / CI-only.** Based on the green F.16.5.4k review journal commit `c7e671b`. No real customer notice delivery, subscription state change, invoice creation, bank payment, or production deployment is part of this milestone.

## Why this step comes before actual holds

The F.16.5.4j policy requires an agreed grace period and a verified prior notice before recommending a hold. The F.16.5.4k journal records *review candidates*, but accepts a supplied evidence object; it is not proof of a delivered notice.

The additive `20261123_028_hold_notice_receipts` migration introduces a tenant-bound table to store future notice delivery evidence:
- Business and current subscription identity
- Delivery provider, SMS/email/WhatsApp channel, unique provider delivery reference
- SHA-256 digest of the provider event, template and accepted-terms versions
- SHA-256 digest of the monthly billing statement associated with the notice
- Delivered/verified timestamps, explicit grace deadline, provenance status (UNVERIFIED by default)

There is a composite FK to the actual business subscription and a unique provider/reference constraint. SQL checks require the grace deadline after delivery and a verified timestamp when provenance is marked VERIFIED. No privileged triggers are used.

## Internal inspection service

`inspectStoredHoldNotice` is a **read-only internal function**, not an HTTP endpoint. It checks:
1. An explicit disposable GitHub Actions MySQL 8.4 environment and CI marker, development mode, and `NOTETO_HOLD_NOTICE_EVIDENCE_ENABLED=true`.
2. Current ACTIVE business and ACTIVE subscription under the existing business/subscription lock order.
3. Authorized business membership and `billing.manage` permission for the reviewer.
4. A tenant-matched receipt with matching statement SHA-256, terms version and delivery time.
5. Recorded provenance, timestamp consistency and whether the *recorded* grace period has elapsed.

Its result always contains `transitionAuthorized:false`, `subscriptionChanged:false` and `paymentInitiated:false`, including when a valid-looking stored receipt and elapsed grace deadline are found.

## Critical remaining trust gap

**There is no authenticated delivery-provider callback adapter.** A table row with `provenance_status='VERIFIED'` is not independently trustworthy: a privileged actor could insert one. The present CI tests insert synthetic receipts to validate data structure and the read-only inspection rules.

Before the table can contribute to real hold decisions, add an authenticated provider callback adapter that validates signature, replay protection, timestamps, delivery semantics, customer recipient, exact notice body/terms, and statement checksum. Store original signed evidence in secure retention storage, use least-privilege DB credentials, and design customer dispute/correction paths.

A separate internal platform reviewer role and trusted ledger/trial/dispute snapshot are also missing. The old review journal must not be turned into a status-changing executor without these.

## CI and release restrictions

The existing `test:mysql-hold-review-journal` suite now includes eight additional notice tests; the disposable migration count rises from 18 to **19**. No application routes or frontend controls are enabled.

Do not migrate a local or production database using this feature branch. CI success means that the schema and isolated test behavior were validated, **not** that customer subscriptions can be held or restored automatically.
